Data Discovery

Find answers across your document archive, with the sources to check them.

Search supported documents and email attachments, or ask a question and follow the citations to the supporting material. Original files stay unchanged, with indexing and retrieval on your own machine.

Useful when the answer could be in a report, a spreadsheet or correspondence filed years ago.

Available for demonstrations and pilot discussions.

What it does

  • Searches Word, Excel, text-layer PDF, plain text and email
  • Finds passages by wording or meaning
  • Answers questions with source citations
  • Saves answers and supporting evidence for later review

Email Archiver

Keep business correspondence with the records it belongs to.

File messages to designated SharePoint or network locations using references such as project and contract numbers. Project offices, accounting teams and other shared services can organize correspondence around the records they manage.

Staff use the filed records in familiar locations. A designated administrator maintains the filing setup and resolves exceptions.

Available for demonstrations and pilot discussions.

Explore Email Archiver

What it does

  • Matches correspondence to configured business records
  • Delivers to SharePoint or network folders
  • Shows which deliveries succeeded or need attention
  • Supports maintenance by a suitably trained power user
