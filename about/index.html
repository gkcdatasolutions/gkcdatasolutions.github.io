About

I’m Gord Cross.

Principal, GKC Data Solutions Inc. · British Columbia, Canada

From a business conversation to working software.

I work with the people who know the business, understand the problem with them, and carry that understanding into the software.

That can mean mapping a process with operations or finance, tracing a rule through a SQL database, or writing the code that connects two applications. Moving between those parts of the work is central to how I approach a project.

Often the starting point is simply that something needs to change. I look at the process and assess existing software before recommending a way forward. If an application on the market meets the need, I recommend buying it.

My work includes enterprise integration, ERP-related data transformation, email filing workflows and local AI-assisted document discovery. The selected projects describe what those systems do.

What matters in the work

GKC is a Canadian consulting and software company. Business analysis and hands-on development are part of the same service.

Put the client’s interests first
I recommend software because it fits the client’s needs. Assessing existing products comes before proposing custom development. The opportunity to write new software is never a reason to choose it.
Understand the exception
The routine path is only part of the process. I want to know what happens when a record is incomplete, a delivery fails or someone has to make a judgement call.
Make decisions traceable
Field mappings and validation rules need to be understandable to the people responsible for them. With AI, that includes access to source evidence and human review.
Keep responsibility clear
Automation needs a clear point of human responsibility. Someone must be able to review an exception and decide what happens next.
Keep the system supportable
I prefer the simplest architecture that meets the requirement. Documentation and diagnostic tools are part of leaving something another person can understand and maintain.
