Gord Cross

Principal, GKC Data Solutions Inc.

Gord’s work sits between business operations and software engineering.

He works with stakeholders to understand workflows, responsibilities, data and business rules, then follows the problem through into process design, integration architecture, databases and working software.

That breadth is particularly useful in projects where the organization knows something needs to change but the answer is not yet clear. The solution may be a better process, a system integration, a custom application, a modernization effort, an AI capability — or a combination of several.

Recent work has included enterprise system integration, ERP-related transformation, operational data synchronization, email and document workflow automation, local AI-assisted document discovery and custom .NET applications.

Gord is comfortable moving between a meeting with operations or finance, a process map, a SQL database, an API specification and production code. That ability to stay connected to both the business intent and technical implementation is central to how GKC works.

How we work

Practical by design.

Start with the business

Understand the workflow and outcome before committing to a technical solution.

Make the rules visible

Important mappings, decisions, validations and responsibilities should be understandable rather than buried in code.

Prefer simple architecture

Use the least complicated solution that satisfies the business, operational and support requirements.

Build for the people operating it

Administration, diagnostics, exceptions and recovery matter as much as the happy path.

Keep humans in control

Automation and AI should reduce work without making important decisions impossible to understand or review.

Leave the system more understandable

Documentation, data models, configuration and operational tooling should make future support easier.

Start with the problem

Start with what needs to work better.

You do not need to know which technology to use. A useful first conversation starts with the work, the people doing it and what is getting in the way.
