Problems we solve

When the process, systems and software no longer fit the business.

Technology problems are often business-process problems in disguise. GKC works with organizations where important work has become difficult, fragmented or unnecessarily manual.

01

Disconnected systems

Information lives in multiple applications. Employees spend too much time reconciling, re-entering or moving it manually.

02

Legacy software

A critical application still works, but the technology underneath it is difficult to support, extend or replace.

03

Manual processes

Spreadsheets, email and tribal knowledge carry workflows that should be clearer, controlled and easier to manage.

04

Difficult integrations

Systems need to exchange data reliably while preserving business rules, validation, traceability and operational control.

05

Unstructured information

Valuable knowledge exists across documents, email, spreadsheets and archives but is difficult to find or use.

06

Practical AI

The organization sees potential in AI but needs a grounded use case, appropriate controls and integration into real work.

Software modernization

Your old software may still contain valuable business logic.

Replacing a legacy application is rarely a simple code conversion. Years of operating knowledge can be buried inside screens, database procedures, reports, validation rules and workarounds.

GKC approaches modernization as both a business-analysis and engineering problem: understand what the application does, determine what still matters, remove what no longer does, and rebuild the right capabilities on a maintainable modern platform.
