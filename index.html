These are the kinds of problems GKC can help untangle.

  • One system has the employee, another has the project, another has the cost code. Someone is keeping them aligned manually.
  • A spreadsheet and a chain of emails have become the approval process. It is hard to see what is waiting or who owns the next step.

How an engagement starts

We begin with a conversation about what is difficult today. Looking at the workflow and the software together helps establish what needs to change, what must keep working and where more investigation is needed.

From there, we agree on a scope and a way forward. For an older application, that includes understanding the business rules already built into it.
