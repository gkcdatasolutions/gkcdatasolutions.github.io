Where the work
gets stuck.

These are the kinds of problems I get called in to untangle.

  • One system has the employee, another has the project, another has the cost code. Someone is keeping them aligned manually.
  • A spreadsheet and a chain of emails have become the approval process. It is hard to see what is waiting or who owns the next step.
  • An application still does an essential job, but every change depends on the few people who understand it.
  • The answer is somewhere in the document archive. Finding it means opening files one by one.

From understanding

How an engagement starts

It starts with a conversation about what is difficult today. Looking at the workflow and the software together establishes what needs to change, what has to keep working, and where I need to dig further.

I then assess available software against those requirements before we agree on a scope and a way forward. For an older application, that includes checking whether an existing product can replace it while preserving the business rules you depend on.
